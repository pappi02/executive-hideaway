# Venue photos

Real photographs of the venue, supplied by the owner (November 2026), enhanced before use:
upscaled 3-4x, colour-corrected (the orange chandelier cast toned down, windows recovered),
and cleaned up. Clean-up edits only — the rooms, furniture and layout are unchanged:

- Christmas trees replaced by potted plants in gold planters
- water dispensers, a hanging ceiling cable, a tissue box and people in the far background removed
- a decanter set placed on the lounge coffee table (`throne-chairs-lounge-decanter`)

The pool and chess photos (`pool-table*`, `pool-rack`, `chess-*`) are enhanced versions of the
October 2026 venue photos previously in `../games/`; `pool-table` also had party balloons removed.

`lounge-pool-wide-landscape.jpg` is the full-size source of the homepage hero and of
`../og-hideaway-lounge.jpg` (see `scripts/generate-og-image.mjs`).
